Three ways to check any insurance broker, including me

  • Look up the NPN. Every licensed producer in the country has one. Mine is 21388659, and you can verify it at NIPR without taking my word for anything.
  • Check your state's Department of Insurance. Florida's public licensee search is run by the Department of Financial Services. Every state has an equivalent. If a producer isn't in it, stop.
  • Call the number and see who answers. Mine rings my actual phone. If you reach a call center, a voicemail tree, or someone who can't tell you their own NPN, you're not talking to who you think you are.

This applies well beyond my own business. If you ever submitted your information to what you thought was my site and then heard from someone else, I'd genuinely like to know — email or call me directly using the details above.

How I'm paid

I am compensated by the insurance carrier when you enroll, not by you. There is no broker fee, no consulting charge, and no markup — a plan costs exactly the same whether you enroll through me, directly with the carrier, or on healthcare.gov. This is worth stating plainly, because "free" in financial services usually isn't.

What that arrangement does mean is that I have an incentive for you to enroll in something. What it does not mean is that I have an incentive to steer you toward any particular carrier — I am independent and not captive to one company. If the honest answer is that you should stay on your employer plan, or that a competitor's product fits you better, that is what I will tell you.
